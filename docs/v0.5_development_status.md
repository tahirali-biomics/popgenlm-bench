## Additional retained workstreams

- **nemo Figure 2 adaptation:** Panel D is a one-fit Arabidopsis development adaptation. Panel A lacks original comparators, maximum-expression results and the source repeated-run protocol. Panel B lacks valid folds and two training/test directions. Panel C lacks paired native *B. napus* observed/predicted held-out values. The source’s ten runs are not replaced by epochs, genes or bootstrap samples. This is not exact reproduction.
- **Original comparator architectures:** nemo90, nemo80, Xpresso, PhytoExpr ensemble/CNN, Basenji-5K, Xpresso without half-life, and PhytoExpr transformer remain in scope. Median and maximum targets, family folds and source-compatible repetitions are required.
- **Botanic ATAC reproduction:** separate from sequence likelihood and expression heads. Missing validated held-out Arabidopsis accessibility inputs/labels, task head or official predictions, matched ChromBPNet outputs and HY5 locus assets block reproduction.
- **GPN-Star:** the TAIR10 checkpoint/alignment are species matched, but checkpoint-generation/calibration provenance and numerical parity remain unresolved. Raw LLR, calibrated signed LLR and supplied calibrated magnitude remain distinct; current ranking is provisional.
- **GPN-MSA:** published human/vertebrate model is not a plant checkpoint. A. lyrata engineering smokes do not establish Arabidopsis/TAIR10 compatibility; compatible plant weights or a separately labelled plant training route are still required.
- **Evaluation:** inspected 300 genes and historical loci remain development data. The 600-gene reserve remains unopened pending a timestamped frozen protocol. New independent variant loci and independent causal-nucleotide truth remain separate requirements.
- **Reproduction:** local notebook/container evidence exists. Public-download notebook execution, Colab preview/hosted execution and an actual independent researcher run are distinct and not all completed.
