# v0.4.0 references and figure provenance

## Models and annotation

- **GPN Brassicales:** Benegas, Batra and Song (2023), *DNA language models are powerful predictors of genome-wide variant effects*, PNAS120:e2311219120. DOI [10.1073/pnas.2311219120](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10622914/). This is the original sequence GPN, not GPN-MSA or GPN-Star.
- **Original PlantCAD / PlantCaduceus:** Zhai et al. (2025), *Cross-species modeling of plant genomes at single-nucleotide resolution using a pretrained DNA language model*. DOI [10.1073/pnas.2421738122](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12184517/). Exact legacy checkpoint is l32; “PlantCAD-S” is retained as a historical alias, not asserted to be a Small checkpoint.
- **PlantCAD2:** Zhai et al. (2026), *PlantCAD2: A DNA foundation model for interpreting genomes across flowering plants*, Cell Genomics,101329. DOI [10.1016/j.xgen.2026.101329](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13576720/).
- **Botanic1:** Barozet et al. (2026), *BOTANIC-1: a series of long-context plant genomic foundation models in the agentic era*, bioRxiv **v3**, DOI [10.64898/2026.09.04.749355](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.04.749355v3.full). Separate requested Fig.4b/4d Arabidopsis ATAC reproduction remains blocked by missing verified measurements, held-out chr4 peaks/split, ATAC head/source predictions and ChromBPNet outputs. The zero-shot512-bp/4-kb figure is a PopGenLM adaptation and does not substitute for these panels. Full v3 caption access was blocked, so panel correspondence is not independently verified.
- **nemo:** Rockenbach et al. (2026), *A deep learning model captures position-specific effects of plant regulatory sequences and suggests genes under complex regulation*, Plant Physiology201:kiag473. DOI [10.1093/plphys/kiag473](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42413033/). **Fig.3a** motivates compatible allele-aligned expression contrasts. Our position-effect figure is a **PopGenLM adaptation** from B.napus transfer predictions, not a rerun of native Arabidopsis validation. Native family-safe weights/folds/scalers and reconciled inputs remain missing.
- **AlphaGenome:** Avsec et al. (2026), *Advancing regulatory variant effect prediction with AlphaGenome*, Nature649:1206–1218. DOI [10.1038/s41586-025-10014-0](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12851941/). **Fig.4b** motivates paired allele tracks and a separate observed-expression panel; **Fig.6b** motivates ALT−REF differences. Our figure is a **PopGenLM adaptation** of these conventions using cached human RNA_SEQ predictions on Arabidopsis sequences. It is neither a source-data redraw of human examples nor a multimodal inference rerun. Exact display track0, version and metadata hash are in the release table.
- **PhyloP method:** Pollard et al. (2010), *Detection of nonneutral substitution rates on mammalian phylogenies*, Genome Research20:110–121. DOI [10.1101/gr.097857.109](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19858363/).
- **Actual plant track:** Tian et al. (2020), *PlantRegMap: charting functional regulatory maps in plants*, NAR48:D1104–D1113. DOI [10.1093/nar/gkz1020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31701126/). Used asset: `Ath_PhyloP.bedGraph.gz` from PlantRegMap's Arabidopsis sequence-conservation download, not a mammalian UCSC track. [Exact URL and digest](../data/benchmarks/v0.4/acquisition.json). Site scores remain site-oriented; exact source alignment taxa/tree are not recovered in the cached metadata.

## Study inputs

- **1001 Genomes Consortium (2016):** *1,135 Genomes Reveal the Global Pattern of Polymorphism in Arabidopsis thaliana*. DOI [10.1016/j.cell.2016.05.063](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27293186/). Used for population variation and genotype/accession identities, v3.1 callset.
- **TAIR10.1 reference and annotation:** NCBI RefSeq assembly [GCF_000001735.4](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/datasets/genome/GCF_000001735.4/), TAIR10.1; accession/version and exact FASTA/GFF hashes are retained. Used for REF validation, context extraction and gene models. This identifies the actual assembly/annotation rather than treating “TAIR10” as a generic interchangeable file.
- **Expression:** Kawakatsu et al. (2016), *Epigenomic Diversity in a Global Collection of Arabidopsis thaliana Accessions*, Cell166:492–505. DOI [10.1016/j.cell.2016.06.044](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27419873/), GEO [GSE80744](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE80744). Used processed normalized gene expression, not inferred RNA-seq coverage. Cod-0 exclusion is documented by the source dataset.
- **cis-eQTL/regulatory source and reporter audit:** Voichek et al. (2024), *Widespread position-dependent transcriptional regulatory sequences in plants*, Nature Genetics56:2238–2246. DOI [10.1038/s41588-024-01907-3](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39266765/). Exact code deposit [Zenodo13170729](https://zenodo.org/records/13170729), DOI10.5281/zenodo.13170729. PopGenLM's accession-split LOCO mapping is recomputed and is not labelled an exact rerun of the published GEMMA/batch analysis. Independent reporter support does not yield an eligible causal-SNP denominator in the frozen pilot.

## Figure classification

Benchmark ranks/recovery, gain/loss loci, PhyloP, GPN, original PlantCAD and PlantCAD2 figures are **PopGenLM adaptations from frozen score/annotation evidence**. Botanic's LLR/context figure is a **frozen PopGenLM adaptation from cached inference**; the public notebook displays its hash-verified image because raw-score transfer terms are unresolved. AlphaGenome/nemo exact panel references and differences are described above. The Arabidopsis expression-by-genotype panel is a **PopGenLM adaptation from processed GSE80744 observations**, not the GTEx coverage panel from Avsec et al. No figure is claimed to be a copied paper panel, a source-data redraw or a new inference rerun.
