# Genomes to AI — PopGenLM Bench v0.4.0

I started with a biological question: can sequence-model evidence help me choose which natural Arabidopsis regulatory variants to investigate after association mapping?

PopGenLM has grown from a verified GPN fixture in v0.1, through a population-aware benchmark in v0.2 and multi-model evolutionary evidence in v0.3, into a regulatory prioritization workflow in v0.4. My engineering work now includes frozen inputs, explicit allele and strand contracts, restartable model scoring, matched evaluation, and shared CPU notebooks that check the results before drawing them.

One decision changed how I interpreted the analysis: I required each model and its baseline to rank exactly the same candidates. A recovery percentage without that contract can reward differences in coverage. With the contract, PlantCAD2-S at 512 bp recovers 18 of 34 held-out association signals versus 17 for the baseline—two gains and one loss. The worked loci show both. Botanic1-S at 4 kb recovers 19/34; AlphaGenome's mammalian-to-plant transfer stays on its own four-locus denominator, and nemo's B. napus transfer remains 8/14 versus 8/14.

Those results justify a transparent way to inspect model-assisted shortlists and plan stronger tests. They do not establish causal regulatory nucleotides. The independent causal-SNP endpoint is still unestimable, and signed rules explored on these loci need independent evaluation. Learning to build reliable scientific AI systems means making that boundary visible—in the code, figures, notebooks, and claims I release.
