Exact source: Living Models / Darween Bioscience SAS, `living-models/Botanic1-S`, revision `813d2d46ca40ab5cd91a6acaa8852d636224992e`. [Pinned license](https://huggingface.co/living-models/Botanic1-S/blob/813d2d46ca40ab5cd91a6acaa8852d636224992e/LICENSE). The exact license copy is supplied in `licenses/Botanic1-LICENSE` in the input bundle, SHA-256 `9276bb71478fc018d9da80ea7345cb882816c37fe027d7d84f1f5a199c60048f`.

**This work uses the Botanic™ model developed by Living Models, available under the Botanic™ Model License.** Cite Barozet et al. (2026), bioRxiv **v3**, DOI [10.64898/2026.09.04.749355](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.04.749355v3.full). The model and outputs are provided as-is without warranties. Modifications: forward/reverse-complement averaging, absolute-value/percentile transformation, fixed combination with an annotation baseline and aggregation into research figures/results. No fine-tuning or model training.

Sections 2(c) and 4 expressly allow research publication of outputs; Sections 3(a–c) require attribution, notices and research-only use. Section 3(d) also contains broad non-transferability language. This release publishes scholarly results/figures under those research-publication provisions but **withholds the raw Botanic score columns and all weights** rather than offering a reusable raw-output dataset under ambiguous transfer terms. The public Botanic notebook checks published rank/count arithmetic and displays the frozen, checksum-verified LLR/context figure; it does not claim to reconstruct Botanic ranks from omitted raw scores. Do not use these results or derivatives for commercial purposes or model distillation/training contrary to the provider terms.

For separate raw reconstruction, obtain the checkpoint at the pinned revision through Hugging Face under its terms and use the fixed 512/4096-bp forward/RC masked-LLR recipe in `scripts/v0.4/score_botanic.py`. The bundle's `restricted_inputs.json` pins the exact expected two-column score-table digest and schema; `load_botanic` verifies those bytes before using them. The full frozen reference/candidate/protocol hashes are retained. Exact original cached outputs require licensor-authorized research sharing; we do not invent a public endpoint or promise byte-identical new GPU inference.

## AlphaGenome

Exact outputs: AlphaGenome 1 API `alphagenome.models.v1`, `ALL_FOLDS`, SDK `0.11.0`, HOMO_SAPIENS RNA_SEQ, all 667 frozen tracks, 131072-bp TAIR10.1 allele sequences. Original cache provenance and track identifiers accompany the bundle. These are **mammalian-to-plant transfer predictions**, not native Arabidopsis expression predictions.

**AlphaGenome outputs and derivatives are subject to the [AlphaGenome Output Terms of Use](https://deepmind.google.com/science/alphagenome/output-terms)** (effective 9 October 2026) and the [Services Additional Terms](https://deepmind.google.com/science/alphagenome/terms) (modified 6 November 2026), inspected for this release. The service terms explicitly permit publishing, sharing and adapting output with clear notice of the output terms and modifications. Research use only; no prohibited commercial, clinical or model-training use. They are not Apache-licensed data. Read the provider terms before reuse; the permissive Atlas-download exception is not claimed for our direct API outputs.

Modifications: central2048-bp crop, all-track mean absolute log1p ALT−REF difference for scoring, fixed percentile/baseline ranking; track index0 selected by metadata order only for the figure. The public bundle includes the 194 aggregate scores and the illustrative paired central track, not all API response arrays. It reproduces cached-score ranks and the displayed track figure, not the full 667-track aggregation for every SNP. The original RAMSES verifier independently checked all194 paired caches. A fresh API response cannot be guaranteed identical because no server weight digest is exposed. Cite Avsec et al. (2026), [10.1038/s41586-025-10014-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-10014-0), Fig.4b/6b for the adaptation.
